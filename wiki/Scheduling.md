# Scheduling

**Control Panel > Scheduling**.

## Clocks

A **clock** is one hour of the programme: a list of rows, each a *song*, *jingle*, *spot break*, *sweeper* and so on, with the category or playlist it takes from. Clocks have randomness, artist separation, next-day separation and restrictive rules; *classic auto* is also available.

## Main auto schedule

![Main auto schedule](images/screens/cp-clocks.png)

The grid has the weekdays and half hours. Pick a **brush** in the palette on the left (a clock or a playlist; the search box finds it; *none* erases) and click or drag over the cells. Click a day name to fill the day, an hour label to fill that hour on every day.

## Playlists

![Playlists](images/screens/cp-playlists.png)

A playlist is a fixed list of songs picked from the library. It plays only at the times you set in the main auto schedule, and a song is used only once per playlist. When all the songs have been played they start again.

## Force clocks

A clock that overrides the schedule for a time (a special show).

## Live rebroadcast

[[Live Rebroadcast]]: a sound card input or an internet stream on the air at set times.

## Final log creator and Log viewer

The **Final log creator** makes the log of what was played; the **Log viewer** shows the history of a day. Reports are in **Utilities > Broadcasting reports**.

![Reports](images/screens/cp-reports.png)

## Station IDs

Make a campaign with the spot of the station ID and give it the priority *1 Station ID* on the break times: it plays first in the break. Or put a *jingle* row in a clock.

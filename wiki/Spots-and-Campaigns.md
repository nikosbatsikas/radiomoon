# Spots and Campaigns

![Spots](images/screens/cp-spots.png)

**Control Panel > Libraries > Spots** (the commercials).

1. Import the spot files.
2. **Add new campaign**: a name, the spots, the **days** and the **times of the breaks** where it plays, and the **priority**. The list of the campaigns shows the times of each one.
3. **Program selected** puts the selected spots on the chosen times; spots that are already there are skipped.

The ad breaks that are coming appear in **Central** in the studio. The priority *1 Station ID* plays first in a break. A **playlister import** brings in campaigns that were made elsewhere.

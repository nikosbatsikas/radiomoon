# Libraries

**Control Panel > Libraries**: Songs, Spots, Jingles, Assets, Sweepers, Voice tracks, Memos, Instant jingle pallets and the Stitcher.

![Songs](images/screens/cp-songs.png)

## Songs

Each song has three categories, vocal, properties, an intro, **mix points**, hooks, volume (gain), song separation (so the same artist is not played too close), a playlister code, and statistics of how often it was played.

- **Import:** a folder of audio files. Options: read the tags, take artist and title from the file name, a category, a gain, a bookmark, **auto cue** (with a level in dB).
- **Edit a song:** the data and the play controls on top, the **waveform** with the mix points below. Preview plays it on the monitor output.
- **Mass change:** change a category, the gain and more for many songs at once; **normalize** sets the volume of the selected songs (on a server it is done on the server, in the background).
- **Delete:** you are asked whether the audio files are deleted too. Deleting items never interrupts the automation unless the deleted item is on the air.

## Spots, jingles, assets, sweepers, voice tracks

Same screens for the other kinds. **Spots** have campaigns: see [[Spots and Campaigns]]. A **sweeper** is a short sound between songs, an **asset** is any other sound, **voice tracks** are the recordings of the presenters. **Memos** are notes that appear in Central.

## Instant jingle pallets

Pallets of buttons for the studio; the colour and the jingle of each button are set with a right-click in the studio.

## Reset database

**Control Panel > Utilities > Reset database** deletes the libraries you select, or everything except the settings.

## Formats

Everything that the audio decoder can read: MP3, AAC / M4A, FLAC, Ogg Vorbis, WAV and more.
